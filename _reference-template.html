Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do emergency callouts?

Yes — most jobs are same-day. Call the number above and describe what's happening; genuine emergencies (no power, sparking, exposed wiring) get priority.

Q. Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully licensed as a QLD electrical contractor and insured for residential and commercial work.

Q. Do you give quotes before starting?

Always. You'll know the price before any work begins — no surprise call-out fees or after-the-fact charges.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Ipswich and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. How quickly can you get here?

Most callouts are same-day. For non-urgent work, Ace will give you a realistic window when you book — not a vague "sometime this week."

Q. Do you charge a call-out fee just to quote?

No. Quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the work is booked in.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — every job is finished to AS/NZS 3000 and backed by workmanship warranty. If something's not right, Ace comes back and fixes it.

Q. Can you help with rental compliance and smoke alarms?

Yes — safety switch and smoke alarm compliance checks for landlords and property managers across Ipswich.
