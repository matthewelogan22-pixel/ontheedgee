Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you give a quote before starting?

Always. Someone comes out, looks at the driveway or site in person, and gives you a written price — no phone-guessed numbers and no surprise costs once the job's underway.

Q. How long before I can drive on a new driveway?

Fresh asphalt needs a few days to properly cure before regular vehicle traffic — you'll get exact timing for your job based on the weather and mix at quote stage.

Q. Do you only lay new driveways, or repair existing ones too?

Both — new asphalt driveways, full resurfacing of tired ones, and targeted crack or pothole repair where the base is still sound.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Cambridge and greater Hobart — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway, larger commercial jobs are worth the drive.

Q. Do you do commercial and car park work?

Yes — resurfacing, patching, and base preparation for car parks and commercial sites, alongside residential driveways.

Q. What are your insurance details?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — send yours through and they'll appear here.

Q. What's the best time of year to reseal a driveway in Tasmania?

Warmer, drier months give the best cure — but get in touch any time and we'll tell you honestly if it's worth waiting a season.

Q. Do you charge a call-out fee just to quote?

No. On-site quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the work is booked in.
