From first lesson to test day.

Four steps, in this order. Nothing to download, nothing to pay up front.

  1. Send a booking request

    Choose a free time above, tell me auto or manual and where to pick you up. Takes about a minute.

  2. I confirm by text, same day

    You'll get a text with the time, the pickup spot and what to bring. Parents can be copied in too.

  3. First lesson on quiet streets

    We start where it's calm: moving off, stopping, steering, checking mirrors. You set the pace, and I log the hours.

  4. Build up to the test route

    Roundabouts, lane changes, parking and busier roads, then practice around the test centre. When you're ready, the test-day package covers the car and a warm-up.

Questions learners and parents ask.

How many logbook hours do I need?

It depends on your state and your age. Your state's transport website has the exact hours.

Which state's rules do you teach?

The rules for the state we're driving in, including the road rules and what the testers look for on the day. Learner and P-plate rules differ a little between states, so if you've moved from interstate, tell me and we'll go over what's changed.

What do I need to bring?

Your learner licence or permit (you can't drive without it), your logbook or logbook app, glasses if your licence says you need them, and comfortable flat shoes. That's it. The car, the L-plates and the dual controls are on me.

What if I need to cancel?

Move or cancel with 24 hours' notice and there's no charge. Cancel on the day or miss the lesson, and it's charged in full, because that hour can't be given to another learner. Sick? Just text me and we'll sort it out.

Can a parent come along?

Of course. Parents are welcome in the back seat for a lesson, and it's a good way to learn how to supervise practice at home between lessons.

Can I use the car for my driving test?

Yes. The test-day package includes a warm-up lesson around the test area, use of the car for the test, and a lift home afterwards.
