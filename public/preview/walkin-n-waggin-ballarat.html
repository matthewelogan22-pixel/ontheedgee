Before you book

Common questions.

Q. Do you walk multiple dogs at once?

Small group walks for dogs that get along well together, or solo one-on-one walks if that suits your dog better — we'll figure out what works at the meet and greet.

Q. Is my dog walked on-leash?

Always on-leash unless we've specifically discussed supervised off-leash time in a secure, agreed area.

Q. Do you do a meet and greet first?

Yes — every new client gets a free meet and greet before the first booking, so your dog is comfortable and you know exactly who's coming.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Wendouree, Lake Wendouree, Ballarat Central and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, message and ask anyway.

Q. Can you pet sit in my home while I'm away?

Yes — drop-in visits or overnight stays, so your pet stays settled in their own environment instead of a kennel.

Q. What are your insurance details?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — send yours through and they'll appear here.

Q. How do I book?

Message the Facebook page or call directly — no app, no booking fee, just a real conversation about what your dog needs.

Q. Do you take last-minute bookings?

Where possible, yes. Message early to lock in regular walks, but last-minute drop-ins can often be fitted in too.
