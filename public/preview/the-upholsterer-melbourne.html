Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you come out to look at the piece, or do I bring it in?

Both work — Paul can quote from photos over the phone, or come out to see larger pieces like lounges and commercial seating in person.

Q. Can you help me pick a fabric or leather?

Yes — Paul brings sample ranges to the quote so you can see and feel options against your existing decor before committing.

Q. Do you repair leather as well as fabric?

Yes, both. Leather tears, cracking and worn armrests can often be repaired without a full re-cover.

Q. How long does a reupholstery job take?

Small repairs can often be turned around in days; full re-covers typically take one to three weeks depending on fabric lead times.

Q. Do you handle commercial jobs like gyms and clinics?

Yes — gym equipment padding, medical furniture and commercial bench seating are recovered in durable, commercial-grade vinyl.

Q. What areas do you service?

Berwick and across Melbourne's south-east, including Pakenham, Cranbourne, Officer, Narre Warren and Beaconsfield.

Q. Is a quote free?

Yes, quotes are free and obligation-free, whether it's from a photo or an in-person look at the piece.
