Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you handle full tree removals or just trimming?

Both — from a light prune of overhanging branches through to complete removal of large, dangerous or dead trees.

Q. What are your insurance details?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — send yours through and they'll appear here.

Q. Do you give a quote before starting?

Always. GMC comes out, has a look at the tree and access, and gives a free written price before anything's cut.

Q. Can you remove the stump as well?

Yes — stump grinding and full stump removal is available as its own job or bundled in with a tree removal.

Q. Do you clean up the branches and mess afterward?

Yes, every job includes clearing green waste — either chipped on-site or hauled away, so the block is left usable.

Q. What areas around Bunbury do you cover?

Bunbury, Pelican Point, Australind, Eaton, Dalyellup, Donnybrook, Capel and the wider South West.

Q. Can you deal with a tree that's come down in a storm?

Yes — GMC handles storm and fallen-tree clean-up, including trees resting on fences, sheds or power lines.
