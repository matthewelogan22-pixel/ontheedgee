Questions

Before you book

What size bin do I actually need?
Tell us the job over the phone — a single room clean-out usually only needs a 2m³, while a full house or reno needs 4-6m³. We'd rather undersell you a bin than have you pay for space you don't use.
Can I get a bin delivered today?
Most of the time, yes, if you call before early afternoon. Bigger reno bins are best booked a day ahead so we can plan the run.
What can't go in the bin?
No asbestos, no liquids, no batteries or gas bottles, and no tyres in a general waste bin. Ask us when you book — most things fall into general or green waste without a problem.
Do you do commercial and mine-site accounts?
Yes — we run ongoing waste accounts for tradies, small sites and Goldfields businesses. Call to set up regular pickups.
Will the bin damage my driveway?
Every bin goes down on protective boards or wheels where needed. Let us know if you've got pavers or a newly laid surface and we'll take extra care.
