Who you're dealing with

You talk to Darren. Darren builds it.

Coastal Cabinets CQ is a local cabinetmaking business working out of Tanby, just behind Yeppoon. Every job runs through the same set of hands — the person who measures your kitchen is the person who cuts it, assembles it and hangs the doors.

That matters more than it sounds. Nothing gets lost between a salesperson and a factory. If a wall's out of square or a benchtop needs to land around an awkward window, it gets solved on the spot instead of turning into a variation three weeks later.

Work spans the Capricorn Coast — new builds and renovations in Yeppoon, Emu Park and the beach suburbs, plus office and shop cabinetry for local businesses. Residential or commercial, it's the same standard and the same free on-site measure to start.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you charge for the measure and quote?

No. The on-site measure and the written quote are free and there's no obligation to go ahead. You only pay once you've accepted a price and the job is booked in.

Q. Are the cabinets actually made locally?

Yes — designed, cut and assembled in the workshop at Tanby, not ordered in as a flat-pack kit and screwed together on site.

Q. Can you replace just the benchtop or just the doors?

Often, yes. If the carcasses are sound, a new benchtop and fresh door fronts can transform a kitchen for a fraction of a full rebuild. Darren will tell you honestly whether it's worth it.

Q. Do you do commercial work?

Yes — reception counters, office storage, workstations and shop joinery for businesses across the Capricorn Coast. Send through plans or a sketch and it can be quoted from that.

Q. How long does a kitchen take?

It depends on the size and what else is happening on site, but you'll get a realistic build-and-install window with the quote — not a vague "few weeks".

Q. Can I pick my own colours and finishes?

Absolutely. Door finishes, benchtop materials, handles and edge profiles are all your call — Darren will bring samples to the measure so you're choosing off the real thing.

Q. Do you work with builders and renovators?

Yes. Cabinetry can be scheduled around the rest of the trades so it lands when the site is ready for it, rather than holding up a hand-over.

Q. What if something isn't right after install?

Call. Doors and drawers settle, and adjusting them is part of the job — Darren comes back and puts it right.

Got a space that needs cabinetry?

Send a photo and rough measurements, or just call and describe it. Either way you'll get a straight answer about what it takes and what it costs.
