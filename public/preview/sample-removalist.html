Questions people ask.

Is my furniture insured during the move?

Removalists aren't required by law to insure your belongings, and many only insure the truck. We'll tell you in writing what our cover includes, and you can arrange your own transit insurance if you want more. On your site: your real answer here.

How far ahead should I book?

Late December and January are the busiest weeks of the year for moves, so book a few weeks ahead for summer. Other times, a week's notice is usually enough.

Do you charge for travel time?

Your written quote shows exactly how travel is charged, before you book.

What if the move takes longer than quoted?

Your quote says how extra time is charged. If something changes on the day (extra items, stairs we didn't know about), we'll tell you before we keep going.

Do I need to pack everything myself?

Up to you. We can pack the whole house, just the fragile things, or drop off boxes so you can do it yourself.
