Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you actually come to me, or do I still need to drive somewhere?

The van comes to you — home, work, or wherever the car's stuck — across Kirrawee and the wider Sutherland Shire.

Q. Do you carry new tyres on the van, or do I need to order first?

Common sizes are carried on the van; less common ones are ordered in first and fitted at your next available slot.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Kirrawee, Sutherland, Miranda, Cronulla, Caringbah, Menai, Engadine, Jannali, Gymea and Loftus — call if you're just outside this list.

Q. Can you fit tyres on 4WDs and light trucks, or just cars?

Cars, 4WDs and light commercial vehicles are all covered, alongside trailer and caravan tyres on request.

Q. Do you do wheel balancing, or just fitting?

Balancing is done on the van as part of every fitting — a wheel doesn't go back on until it's balanced.

Q. How quickly can you get to me?

Most bookings are same-day — call in the morning for an afternoon slot in the Shire.

Q. Is there an extra charge just for the mobile callout?

The price you're quoted for the tyre and fitting is the price you pay — no separate call-out fee within the service area.

Q. Can you supply new wheels as well as tyres?

Yes — as a Dynamic Wheel Co. dealer, steel and alloy rims can be supplied and fitted in the same visit.

Stuck with a flat right now?

Call, and the van will be pointed your way — Kirrawee and the Sutherland Shire, same-day on most jobs.
