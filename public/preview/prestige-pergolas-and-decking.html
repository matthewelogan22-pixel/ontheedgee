Before you message

Common questions.

Q. Do you build both timber and composite decking?

Yes — hardwood and merbau for a classic look, or composite boards if you want less ongoing maintenance. Happy to talk through which suits your block.

Q. Do you handle council permits for decks and pergolas?

We'll tell you upfront whether your job needs a permit and point you in the right direction — most standard decks and pergolas are straightforward.

Q. How long does a typical deck or pergola take?

Depends on size and weather, but most standard jobs run one to two weeks once materials are on site. You'll get a realistic timeframe with your quote.

Q. Do you charge for quotes?

No — site visits and quotes are free, with no obligation to book anything in.

Q. What areas around Bendigo do you cover?

Bendigo and the surrounding Goldfields suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, message anyway.

Q. Can you match an existing deck or extend one?

Yes — extensions and repairs to existing decks are common jobs, not just new builds from scratch.

Q. What's the difference between a pergola and a patio, really?

Broadly, a pergola has an open or slatted roof for filtered shade, while a patio or alfresco has a solid roof for full weather cover. We'll help you pick based on how you'll use the space.

Q. How do I get in touch?

Message the Facebook page directly — that's the fastest way to reach us and get a site visit booked in.
