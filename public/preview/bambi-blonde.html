Before you book

Common questions.

Q. My hair is dark — can you take me blonde?

Usually, yes, though it may take more than one appointment to lift safely without wrecking the condition. That gets mapped out at your free consult.

Q. Can you fix colour done somewhere else?

Colour correction is one of the specialties here. Bring a photo of the current colour and what you're after and it can be properly assessed first.

Q. Do you offer free colour consultations?

Yes, always, before any colour service — especially important for a big change or a correction.

Q. How long does a balayage appointment take?

Generally 2.5 to 3.5 hours depending on length and how much lift is needed, plus toning and a finish.

Q. How far in advance should I book?

A couple of weeks for a standard colour, longer for a full colour correction or around formal season.

Q. Do you use professional products?

Yes — salon-grade lightener, toner, and bond-repair treatments on every colour service, not supermarket brands.

Q. Do you also do cuts and styling?

Yes — precision cuts and blow dry styling, usually paired with a colour appointment but also available on their own.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — if a tone isn't sitting right within a few days of a colour service, get in touch and it'll be looked at.
