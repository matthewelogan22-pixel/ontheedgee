Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you install split systems and ducted air con?

Both — from a single split system in a bedroom to a full ducted system for the whole house. Dempsey will recommend what actually suits the room, not just what's easiest to sell.

Q. What are your licence and insurance details?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — send yours through and they'll appear here.

Q. Do you give quotes before starting?

Always. You'll know the price before any work begins — no surprise call-out fees or after-the-fact charges.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Toowoomba and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. My unit isn't cooling properly — is that a quick fix?

Often, yes. Low gas, a dirty filter, or a faulty sensor are common and quick to sort. Call and describe what's happening and Dempsey can usually tell you what's likely on the phone.

Q. Do you charge a call-out fee just to quote?

No. Quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the work is booked in.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — every install and repair is backed by workmanship warranty. If something's not right, Dempsey comes back and fixes it.

Q. How often should a split system be serviced?

Generally once a year, ideally before the start of summer, to keep it running efficiently and avoid a breakdown on the first 35-degree day.
