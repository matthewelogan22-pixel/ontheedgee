Before you message

Common questions.

Q. Do you install pool safety fencing?

Yes — new pool fencing and bringing existing fences up to current compliance, gates and latches included.

Q. Do you work on rural blocks outside Darwin City?

Yes, chain wire and boundary fencing for rural properties around Humpty Doo, Howard Springs and Litchfield.

Q. How do I get a quote?

Send a message on Facebook with the job and, if you can, a photo of the fence line or block — a time gets arranged to look at it in person.

Q. Do you charge for quotes?

No, on-site quotes are free — you get a firm price before anything's booked in.

Q. Can you match an existing fence line?

Yes — extensions and repairs are matched to existing panels and posts where possible.

Q. How does fencing hold up in the wet season?

Fixings and materials are chosen specifically for Top End conditions — monsoon rain, humidity and termites all factor into what goes in the ground.

Q. Do you repair storm-damaged fences?

Yes — damaged panels, posts and gates repaired or replaced without redoing sections that are still solid.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Darwin City and the surrounding suburbs and rural fringe — see the service area above. Just outside it? Send a message and ask.
