Finish standards

The finish is specified, not guessed.

Plasterboard finish levels decide how a wall looks once the lights go on. TCP quotes to the level the job actually needs — and says so when a higher one is worth paying for.

Level 3

Heavy texture & back of house

Where a heavy wall covering or texture finish is going on, or in plant and storage areas that will never sit under critical light.

Level 4

The usual standard

The everyday finish for homes, offices and retail — flat joints and fasteners, ready for flat paint or light wall coverings.

Level 5

Critical lighting

Full skim coat for gloss paint, long wall runs, and anywhere raking light or big windows will show every imperfection.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you take on small domestic jobs, or only commercial?

Both. The commercial side is the bigger part of the business, but a cracked ceiling or a patch after a burst pipe is still a normal day’s work. Call and describe it.

Q. How long has the business been going?

Since 1986, starting as Paul Plastering Service and trading as Toowoomba Commercial Plastering from 2004. Same family, same town.

Q. Can you quote from drawings?

Yes. Send the architectural set with the partition and ceiling schedules and you get a measured take-off rather than a guess.

Q. Do you install fire-rated systems?

Yes — passive fire wall and ceiling systems installed to the specified system, for tenancies and buildings that need the rating.

Q. What finish level should I ask for?

Level 4 covers most homes and offices. If the walls sit under raking light, run long, or are getting gloss paint, Level 5 is worth the extra. TCP will tell you which one the job needs.

Q. Can you work around a trading business?

Yes. Staged work and out-of-hours sheeting in occupied shops and offices are part of commercial fit-out — the area gets sealed off and handed back clean.

Q. Do you do ornamental and period-style work?

Yes — decorative cornice, ceiling roses and arches, including repairs and profile matching on older Toowoomba homes.

Q. How far out of Toowoomba will you travel?

Regularly out to Highfields, Gatton, Helidon, Dalby and Warwick. If you are further out than that, call anyway and ask.
