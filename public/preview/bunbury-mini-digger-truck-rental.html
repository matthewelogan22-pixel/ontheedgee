Before you book

Common questions.

Q. What's the difference between wet hire and dry hire?

Wet hire means the machine arrives with an operator who runs it for you. Dry hire means you get the machine on its own and operate it yourself. Both are available here — pick whichever suits the job and your crew.

Q. Can you get a machine down the side of my house?

Tight access is most of the work. Measure the narrowest point — usually a side gate or a path between the house and the fence — and call it through, and you'll get a straight answer on whether it fits before anything is booked.

Q. Do you take the dirt away as well?

Yes. The tipper truck can be booked alongside the excavator, so the spoil leaves the site as it's dug instead of sitting on your verge for a week.

Q. Do you deliver the machine or do I collect it?

Machines are delivered and picked up — you don't need a float or a trailer rated for it. Delivery is factored into the price you're quoted.

Q. Do you work with builders and trades, or just homeowners?

Both. Builders, landscapers, fencers, plumbers and reticulation crews hire the machines regularly, and homeowners book wet hire for one-off backyard jobs.

Q. What concrete work do you do?

Concrete services run alongside the earthworks — site prep and excavation through to the pour, so the digging and the slab aren't split between two contractors.

Q. How far ahead do I need to book?

It depends on the week. Call as early as you can — especially if you're coordinating a concrete pour or a trade behind you — and you'll get a realistic date rather than a hopeful one.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Bunbury, Eaton, Australind, Capel and Dardanup. Just outside that? Call and ask — it often still works.
