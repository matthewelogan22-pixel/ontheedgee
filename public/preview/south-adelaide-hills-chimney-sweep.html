Who's behind it

A slow combustion specialist, not a generalist with a brush.

South Adelaide & Hills Chimney Sweep Service focuses on one thing: keeping slow combustion fireplaces and open chimneys safe to run through an Adelaide Hills winter. That means clearing creosote build-up before it becomes a fire risk, checking dampers and seals, and flagging anything — cracked flue liners, blocked cowls, nesting birds — before it turns into a bigger job.

It's the kind of work homeowners across the southern suburbs and up through the Hills book every autumn, usually word of mouth or off a Facebook post — because right now, that's the only place to find this business online.

Why it matters

Creosote build-up is the leading cause of preventable chimney fires.

A yearly sweep isn't just tidy housekeeping — it's the difference between a fireplace that's safe to run all winter and one that isn't.

Creosote removed
Build-up that fuels chimney fires is cleared from the flue walls, not just brushed past.
Blockages checked
Birds' nests, leaf litter and debris found and cleared before they trap smoke and gas indoors.
Seals & dampers inspected
Slow combustion door seals and dampers checked so heaters run efficiently, not just cleaned.

Before you message

Common questions.

Q. Do you service wood heaters and open fireplaces?

Both — slow combustion wood heaters are the speciality, but open fireplace chimneys are swept and inspected too.

Q. How often should a chimney actually be swept?

Once a year is the general rule for a fireplace used regularly through winter — ideally before the first cold snap, while creosote build-up is still easy to clear.

Q. Do you cover the Adelaide Hills as well as South Adelaide?

Yes — from the southern suburbs up through Blackwood and into the Hills towns like Stirling, Aldgate, Mount Barker and Hahndorf.

Q. Do you have a website, or just Facebook?

Just Facebook for now — send a message with your suburb and a photo of the fireplace and a price comes back directly.

Q. Is a sweep worth it if the fire only gets used occasionally?

Yes — creosote and blockages build up regardless of how often the fire runs, and a light-use fireplace is often the one that gets forgotten until something goes wrong.

Q. Is there a call-out fee just to get a quote?

No — send through a photo and your suburb and a price comes back before anything is booked.
