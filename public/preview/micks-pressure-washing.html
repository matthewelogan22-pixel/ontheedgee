Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Will pressure washing damage my driveway or pavers?

No — Mick matches the pressure and technique to the surface. Older or softer surfaces get a gentler approach so nothing is etched or stripped away.

Q. Do you use chemicals?

Only where needed, and always surface-safe. Most driveway and path jobs are handled with water pressure alone.

Q. How much does a driveway clean cost?

It depends on size, surface type and how much build-up there is. Send a photo for a free, no-obligation quote before anything is booked.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Ballarat East and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, message and ask anyway.

Q. How quickly can you get here?

Most bookings are turned around within the same week. Mick will give you a realistic window when you book — not a vague "sometime soon."

Q. Do you charge just to quote?

No. Quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the job is booked in.

Q. Are you insured?

Yes, fully covered by public liability insurance for residential and commercial work.

Q. Can you clean roofs and gutters too?

Yes — moss, lichen and leaf build-up on roofs and gutters, cleared as part of a full exterior clean or on its own.
