What we do

Three things, done properly.

Not a bit of everything — lawns, gardens and gutters, which between them cover most of what a tablelands property actually needs.

01

Lawns

Mowing, edging and line trimming, front and back. Cut height set to the season — shorter through the growing months, left longer heading into frost so the grass isn't stressed going into winter.

02

Gardens

Weeding, pruning, hedge shaping and general tidy-ups. Beds cleared by hand, and cuttings taken away rather than left stacked behind the shed.

03

Gutters

Gutters and downpipes cleared by hand and flushed so you can see they're running. Armidale's deciduous trees fill them fast, and a blocked gutter in a tablelands downpour backs straight up under the eaves.

Questions

Common questions.

When should gutters be done around here?

Once the autumn drop has finished and before the first heavy rain. Properties under elms or oaks often need a second look partway through the drop.

Do you take the green waste away?

Yes — clippings, prunings and what comes out of the gutters go with us rather than getting left in a pile.

Can I get the lawn done regularly?

Yes. Most places go on a regular round through the growing season and ease off over the colder months. One-off jobs are fine too.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually, as long as there's access and any gates are unlocked. Plenty of jobs happen while people are at work.

How do I get a price?

Call or text 0493 129 179 with the address and what you need. A photo helps and often saves a trip out.

Through the year

What needs doing, and when.

The tablelands run to their own calendar. Frost late, growth hard and short, and a leaf drop that fills every gutter in town.

Sep – Nov

Growth

Once the frosts let go it comes on fast. Regular rounds start back up.

Dec – Feb

Peak

Longest grass of the year. Fortnightly cuts, and hedges shaped before they get away.

Mar – May

The drop

Elms and oaks let go. Gutters fill in a fortnight — this is the window to get them cleared.

Jun – Aug

Frost

Little to mow. Structural pruning, and a second gutter check after the winter storms.
