About KSM

A local sign shop that's part of Katherine.

Dean Hersey and Mark Parker run Katherine Sign Management out of Katherine East — a genuinely local, Territory-owned sign shop, not a Darwin outfit that occasionally drives down. Locals, businesses, government departments, non-government organisations and sporting clubs across the region have relied on the team for wide-format printing, vinyl cut lettering, vehicle signage and window tint for decades.

In 2017 the team built a fully sealed, air-conditioned tinting and wrap-install booth on site — meaning dust-free, temperature-controlled application for vehicle wraps, tint and vinyl lettering, all year round, even through the build-up.

Frequently Asked

Questions we get around the workshop.

Q. How long does vehicle signage take?

Simple ute door decals can often be turned around in a few days. Full wraps and multi-vehicle fleet jobs take longer — Dean or Mark will give you a realistic timeframe once they've seen the job and the vehicle.

Q. Do you travel out to stations and remote properties?

Yes — for larger signage jobs (site signage, gate signs, larger installs) we can arrange to come to you across the Katherine region. Vehicle wraps and tinting are best done in the sealed workshop on Palmer Street.

Q. What's the difference between a partial and a full wrap?

A partial wrap or vehicle signage covers key panels — doors, bonnet, tailgate — with your logo and details. A full wrap covers the entire vehicle in printed vinyl. We'll talk through what suits your budget and how much visibility you're after.

Q. Does your window tint meet legal requirements?

Yes — vehicle tint is applied to comply with current regulations, and we'll talk you through the legal limits for your vehicle before any film goes on.

Q. Can you handle signage for government or council tenders?

Yes — KSM regularly supplies signage to local government, non-government organisations and sporting bodies across the region, and can work to tender specs and timeframes.

Q. Do I need to pay a deposit?

For most standard jobs, no deposit is needed — you'll get a written quote up front and pay on completion. Larger custom jobs may require a deposit, which we'll confirm before starting.
