Before you call

Common questions.

Q. My pool has gone green — how fast can you fix it?

Most green pool recoveries clear up within a few days of treatment, depending on how far gone it is. Call and describe the colour and how long it's been neglected for a realistic timeline.

Q. How often should my pool actually be serviced?

Most Adelaide backyard pools do well on a weekly visit over summer and fortnightly over cooler months — Pool Care Specialist will recommend a schedule based on your pool and how it's used.

Q. Do you supply the chemicals?

Yes — chemicals are dosed and included as part of the service based on what the water test actually shows, not a flat guess.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Metro Adelaide, with a focus on the northern and north-eastern suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. Can you fix a broken pump or filter?

Minor equipment issues and filter cleans are handled directly. Bigger equipment replacements can be quoted on inspection.

Q. Do you do one-off cleans, or only ongoing contracts?

Both. One-off cleans for a special occasion or a house sale, or an ongoing weekly/fortnightly visit — whatever suits.

Q. Do you service rental properties for landlords?

Yes — regular servicing and reporting for landlords and property managers who need the pool kept swim-ready between tenants.

Q. What if I'm going away and need the pool checked?

One-off holiday checks and top-ups can be arranged so the pool isn't a green mess when you get back.
