Every step, written on your quote.

A restoration is six jobs done in order. The quote lists each one, so you can see what you're paying for before anyone starts.

  1. Roof check and photo report

    We go up, photograph what we find, and send you the photos with the quote.

  2. Broken tiles replaced

    Cracked or slipped tiles swapped out, matched as closely as we can.

  3. Cleaned

    Moss, lichen and grime removed so the new coating sticks. Asbestos roofs are never pressure-cleaned.

  4. Ridges rebedded and repointed

    Bedding is the cement mix that holds the ridge caps on. Pointing is the flexible layer on top that seals them.

  5. Sealed and coated

    A primer or sealer, then the coating, in the colour you picked.

  6. After photos

    Gutters cleared of our mess, and a set of after photos sent to you.

Six things to check, whoever you hire.

Door-knock roofing scams keep making the news. These checks take a few minutes.

A licence you can look up

In NSW, roof tiling over $5,000 needs a licence, and the licence number has to be on the roofer's ads and website. Check it on the state's public register.

A licence you can look up

In Queensland, roof tiling over $3,300 needs a licence, and the licence number has to be on the roofer's ads and website. Check it on the state's public register.

A licence you can look up

Ask for the roofer's licence or registration number and check it on your state's public register.

A written quote, step by step

What's being repaired, how it's cleaned, what coating goes on, and how many coats. A single figure isn't a quote.

Photos of your own roof

You can't see your roof from the ground. Ask for photos of the damage before you agree to fix it.

A safety plan

Work where someone could fall more than 2 metres is high-risk construction work. It needs a written safety plan (a SWMS) before anyone goes up.

Asbestos

Homes built before 1990 may have asbestos cement roofing. Using a high-pressure water cleaner on asbestos is illegal in every state. If your roof might be asbestos, ask how they'll clean it.

If they knocked on your door

You get 10 business days to change your mind. In that time they can't start the work or take any money, except for emergency repairs.

Sources: NSW Government (licensing and advertising), QBCC (licensing and advertising), Safe Work Australia (high-risk construction work), Asbestos and Silica Safety and Eradication Agency (asbestos cement roofs), ACCC (door-to-door sales).

Questions people ask.

Could my roof be asbestos?

If your home was built before 1990 and the roof is cement sheeting rather than tiles or metal, it could be. We'll tell you what we think it is after the roof check. We never pressure-clean an asbestos roof, because it releases fibres and it's illegal. On your site: what you do with asbestos roofs, or who you refer them to.

How long does a restoration take?

It depends on the size of the roof, the repairs and the weather, because coatings need dry days. Your quote gives the expected days. On your site: your usual timeframe.

Do I need to be home?

Only for the roof check, if you'd like to see the photos with us. After that, we just need access to the yard and an outdoor tap.

What warranty do I get?

The warranty is written into your quote: what it covers, for how long, and who backs it. On your site: your real workmanship warranty and the coating maker's warranty.

Someone knocked and said my roof needs work. What should I do?

You don't have to decide on the spot, and you can ask them to leave. If you sign with a door-knocker, you have 10 business days to change your mind, and they can't start or take money in that time. Get a second opinion with photos.
