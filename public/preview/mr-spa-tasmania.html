Before you message

Common questions.

Q. Do you do one-off cleans or only ongoing service?

Both — a single clean before a summer of guests, or a regular schedule so it's never left to go green.

Q. Can you fix a pool that's turned green?

Yes — green pool recovery is one of the most common calls, especially coming out of a Tasmanian winter.

Q. Do you service spas as well as pools?

Yes, spa cleaning, chemical balancing and repairs are covered alongside pool work.

Q. Do you repair pumps and filters, or just replace them?

Repairs first where possible — equipment is diagnosed properly before anything's replaced unnecessarily.

Q. How do I get a quote?

Send a message on Facebook or call 0427 749 140 with a bit of detail on the pool or spa — pricing follows from there.

Q. Can you help open the pool for summer?

Yes — seasonal opening (and closing going into winter) is a regular part of the work.

Q. Do you sell pool equipment as well as service it?

Yes, pumps, filters and general pool accessories are available and fitted on-site.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Blackmans Bay, Kingston and greater Hobart — see the service area above. Just outside it? Send a message and ask.
