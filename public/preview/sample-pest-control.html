Why a yearly inspection is worth it.

Termites usually work out of sight, inside timber. Here's what's worth knowing before you find any.

Your insurance may not cover it

Many home insurance policies exclude termite damage. Suncorp, for example, says:

"Suncorp Insurance doesn't cover damage caused by insects, vermin or rodents."

Check your own policy.

Once a year is the usual advice

Most homes should be inspected at least once a year. Suncorp's own tip is regular pest inspections, "ideally annually".

What we check

The roof void, subfloor, walls, fences, trees and garden edges: anywhere termites travel or feed. Everything we find goes in a written report with photos.

On your site: your tools, and the standard you inspect to (for example AS 3660.2), only if you do.

Does home insurance cover termite damage?

Often not. Many policies exclude damage caused by insects, including termites, so check yours.

How often should I get a termite inspection?

Most homes should be inspected at least once a year. Your inspector may suggest more often if there's a risk nearby.
