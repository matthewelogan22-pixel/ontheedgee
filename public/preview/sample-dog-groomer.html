A small deposit holds your spot.

Booking takes about as long as ordering a coffee. The deposit is what makes the whole thing work.

Before you book.

My dog's coat is matted. Can you still groom them?

Yes. Light knots can usually be brushed out. Tight matting close to the skin is kinder to clip short and start fresh, because brushing it out hurts. We'll show you and agree on it before we start.

What should I expect at my puppy's first groom?

A short, gentle visit: a warm bath, a quiet dry, a nail trim and a face tidy, with plenty of breaks and treats. The aim is a puppy who's happy to come back, not a perfect haircut on day one.

My dog gets anxious. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Let us know when you book and we'll give them a quieter time slot, go slower, and stop for breaks. Nervous dogs often do better with shorter, more regular visits.

Do you groom cats?

Salty Paws is dogs only. On your site, this answer says whatever's true for you, and if you do cats they get their own spot on the price list.

What if I need to cancel?

Move or cancel with 24 hours' notice and your deposit carries over to the new booking. Cancel on the day or don't show, and the deposit covers the empty spot.

How long does a groom take?

Most small dogs are ready in about two hours; big fluffy dogs can take three. We'll text you when they're ready for pick-up.
