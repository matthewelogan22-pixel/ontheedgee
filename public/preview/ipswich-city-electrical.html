Warning signs

When it's worth picking up the phone.

None of these fix themselves, and three of the four get worse cheaply now and expensively later.

Sign 01

Breaker keeps tripping

Something on that circuit is drawing more than it should, or there's a fault. Resetting it repeatedly doesn't address either.

Sign 02

Warm switchboard

A board or outlet that's warm to touch, or smells faintly of hot plastic, needs looking at now rather than next month.

Sign 03

Lights flickering

Usually a loose connection somewhere. Harmless-looking, and one of the more common causes of electrical fires.

Sign 04

No safety switches

If the board has fuses rather than RCDs, nothing is protecting anyone from a shock. Common in pre-90s Ipswich homes.
