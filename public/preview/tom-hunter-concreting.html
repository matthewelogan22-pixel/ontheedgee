About

Local hands, local ground knowledge

Tom Hunter Concreting started the way most good tradie businesses do — word of mouth around Mount Gambier and the Limestone Coast. What began as small path and slab jobs has grown into a trusted name for driveways, shed bases, patios and landscaping prep across the district.

Being local means knowing how Mount Gambier's clay-over-limestone ground behaves through wet winters and dry summers — so every job gets the right base prep, reinforcement and finish before a drop of concrete goes down.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you give free quotes?

Yes — Tom comes out to measure the job on-site and gives you a straight price before anything is booked in.

What areas do you cover?

Mount Gambier and the surrounding Limestone Coast towns, including Yahl, Suttontown, Millicent and Penola.

How long before I can use new concrete?

Concrete is generally safe to walk on within a couple of days and ready for vehicle traffic after about a week, weather depending — you'll get exact timing for your job.

Do you handle exposed aggregate and coloured finishes?

Yes, alongside standard broom and steel-trowel finishes, for driveways, patios and paths.
