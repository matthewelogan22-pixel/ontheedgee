Who's behind it

Thirty years of workshop and construction know-how, now parked in your driveway.

WelDone Mobile Welding started back in 1996 in engineering workshops and on construction sites around Perth. After years running the business in the metro area, the rig moved south — now WelDone is based in South Bunbury and works right across the South West.

Instead of you loading a trailer and driving to a workshop, the workshop comes to you: general fabrication, truck and trailer repairs, farm and ag equipment, gates, and rust repair on classic cars, all handled on-site with a properly equipped mobile welding rig.

Before you message

Common questions.

Q. Do you actually come to my property?

Yes — WelDone is 100% mobile. The rig comes to your home, farm or job site anywhere across Bunbury and the South West, so there's no need to load anything onto a trailer.

Q. What sort of jobs do you take on?

General welding and fabrication, truck and trailer repairs, farm and ag equipment, gates and fencing, rust repair on classic cars, and custom ute trays and steel work.

Q. Can you quote from a photo?

Often, yes. Send a photo and a description on Facebook and you'll get a straight answer — if it needs a proper look, an on-site visit gets booked in.

Q. How far do you travel?

Based in South Bunbury, covering Australind, Eaton, Dalyellup, Donnybrook, Collie and out to Busselton — get in touch if you're further afield.

Q. Do you work on old cars and rust jobs?

Yes — rust repair and metalwork on classic cars and utes is a regular part of the work, done carefully because these jobs matter to the owner.

Q. How experienced are you?

WelDone has been welding and fabricating since 1996, with years in Perth engineering workshops and construction before setting up mobile in the South West.

Q. How do I book a job in?

Send a message on Facebook with what needs doing and roughly where you're based, and a time gets locked in from there.

Q. Is there a call-out fee just for a quote?

No — a price gets worked out from your photo and description first, before anything is booked in.

Got something that needs fixing on-site?

Ute tray, farm gate, cracked trailer or a rust job that's been sitting in the shed — send a photo and get a price back.
