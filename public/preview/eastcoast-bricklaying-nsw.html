Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you work with builders on new builds?

Yes — boundary and base-course blockwork for new-build sites is regular work. Call or email with the plans and Eastcoast will quote from there.

Q. Do you do small jobs like repairs and letterboxes?

Yes — no job is too small to quote properly, from a single repointing job to a full retaining wall.

Q. Do you give quotes before starting?

Always. You'll know the price before any work begins — no surprise charges once the job is underway.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Newcastle and Lake Macquarie — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. How do I get a quote?

Call or email with a description of the job — plans or photos help but aren't essential. Eastcoast will assess it and give you a written price.

Q. Do you charge a call-out fee just to quote?

No. Quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the work is booked in.

Q. Can you match existing brickwork on an older home?

Matching brick colour, bond pattern and mortar joint on extensions and repairs is part of the job — it's discussed at quote stage.

Q. Are you licensed and insured?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — ask when you call, or send them through and they'll be added here.
