Before you book

Common questions.

Q. How long does a carpet take to dry?

Most rooms are walkable within a few hours and fully dry the same day, depending on airflow and carpet type.

Q. Do you do upholstery as well as carpet?

Yes — lounges, sofas and fabric furniture are cleaned in place using the same hot water extraction process.

Q. Can you meet a real estate end-of-lease standard?

Yes — carpets and full property cleans are booked and timed to meet handover inspections.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Devonport and the surrounding North-West Coast towns — see the service area above. Just outside it? Ask anyway.

Q. Do you give a quote before the job?

Always — a straightforward price based on rooms or items, agreed before any booking is confirmed.

Q. Can you remove pet stains and odours?

In most cases, yes — treatments are applied as part of the steam clean for stains and odours, not charged as a separate mystery fee.

Q. Do you clean offices as well as homes?

Yes — office and builders cleaning are available alongside residential carpet and upholstery work.

Q. How far in advance should I book?

A few days' notice is usually enough, but end-of-lease jobs are best booked as soon as your move-out date is set.
