What we do

Homes, and the businesses in between.

Most of it is domestic — the sort of work that makes a house safer to live in rather than newer to look at.

Switchboard upgrades

Ceramic fuses replaced with a modern board, breakers and RCDs on every circuit, tested and documented on completion.

Rewiring

Full or partial rewires for older Ipswich homes. Can be staged so you're not living in a construction site for a fortnight.

Safety switches

RCDs fitted and tested. The single cheapest thing you can do to make an older house safer.

Lighting

LED conversions, downlights, outdoor and sensor lighting — including getting the old halogens out of the roof space.

Power points & fans

Extra outlets, USB points, ceiling fans, exhaust fans. The small list that's been on the fridge for a year.

Fault finding

Tripping breakers, dead circuits, lights that flicker. Worth diagnosing properly rather than guessing at.

Around the house

Where the work usually is.

In a typical older Ipswich home, it's nearly always one of these four.

Outside

The switchboard

If it still has ceramic fuses and no RCDs, that's the first job worth doing and the one that matters most.

Kitchen

Not enough outlets

Double adaptors and extension leads behind the bench usually mean the circuit was never designed for what's on it now.

Roof space

Old halogens

They run hot, sit against insulation and eat power. LED replacement is quick and pays for itself.

Bathroom

Exhaust fans

A fan that doesn't clear steam properly turns into mould on the ceiling, and that's a bigger job later.
