Who's behind it

A local Toowoomba blinds and awnings specialist, not a franchise call centre.

AK Blinds & Awnings has spent the last several years measuring, building and fitting Ziptrak awnings, roller and roman blinds, shutters and security screens for homes across Toowoomba, Highfields and the Darling Downs.

Every job starts with a free, in-home measure and quote — the same person who quotes the job is the one who turns up to install it. No subcontractors, no guesswork.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do free quotes?

Yes — AK comes out to measure the space in person and gives you a written price. No cost, no obligation to book.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Toowoomba and the surrounding Darling Downs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. Do you install Ziptrak awnings?

Yes — Ziptrak track-guided awnings are one of AK's main installs, along with outdoor PVC blinds and fixed awnings.

Q. Can you fix an existing awning or blind instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. Sticking tracks, torn fabric or a broken mechanism can usually be repaired rather than needing a full replacement.

Q. How long does an install take?

Most residential jobs — a Ziptrak awning, an outdoor blind, or a set of shutters — are fitted in a day once fabric and hardware are in.

Q. Do you do security doors and screens too?

Yes, alongside blinds and awnings, AK measures and fits security doors and screens to match the home.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — every install is measured and fitted properly, with AK standing behind the workmanship.

Q. What's the best way to get in touch?

Call 0428 262 306, or send a message on the AK Blinds & Awnings Facebook page — both get a direct reply, no call centre.
