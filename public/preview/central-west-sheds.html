About the business

A Parkes shed builder who stays on the job until the last screw is in.

Central West Sheds has been building sheds for over thirty years. It is a family-run outfit working out of Maguire Road in Parkes, and the same people who quote your shed are the ones who turn up to put it up.

The point of difference is simple: you deal with one builder for the whole thing. Council paperwork, site set-out, slab, frame, cladding, doors, flashings — it all comes back to us, so there is nobody to chase and nothing that falls between two trades.

That covers everything from a two-bay carport beside the house to a fabricated truss machinery shed out on the paddock, with lean-tos, insulation and whatever door setup the job calls for.

Questions we get asked

Before you ring, here is the short version.

Do you sort out the council approval?

Yes. We do the entire job from start to finish, and that includes the council approval. You do not have to lodge anything or work out which forms your council wants.

How big can you go?

Any size, from a carport up to a fabricated truss shed. Tell us the span you need clear, the height you need under the gutter and what is going inside, and we will work back from there.

Can you add a lean-to to my existing shed?

Lean-tos are a regular part of what we build, both on new sheds and off the side or gable end of one that is already standing. We will come and look at how the existing shed is framed first.

Do you do the slab as well?

Site works and concrete are part of the start-to-finish job. Ring us with the location and what the ground is like and we will talk you through what the site needs.

Can the shed be insulated?

Yes — roof only, or roof and walls. It makes a real difference in a workshop or anywhere you are storing gear that does not like the heat.

How do I get a price?

Ring 0427 378 529 or email centralwestsheds@bigpond.com with the rough size, where you are and what the shed is for. If it is easier, send a photo of the spot.
