About New Age Gutter Cleaning

A local South West outfit that actually clears the whole gutter.

New Age Gutter Cleaning Service is a locally owned and operated business working across Bunbury and the surrounding South West suburbs. No franchise call centre, no upsell — just a proper clean of every metre of gutter, not just what's visible from the ground.

Between the peppermint trees and jarrah in Bunbury's older streets, gutters here fill up fast. A blocked downpipe in winter means water finding its way somewhere you don't want it — under the eaves, into the ceiling cavity, or pooling against the foundations.

Common questions

What people ask before booking.

Q. How often should gutters actually be cleaned?

Most Bunbury homes under trees need a clean at least once a year, ideally before the winter storms roll in.

Q. Do you clear the downpipes too, or just the gutters?

Both — a gutter that's clear but a downpipe that's blocked will still overflow, so downpipes are checked and flushed every time.

Q. Is it safe on a two-storey house?

Yes — proper ladder setup and safety gear is used for single and two-storey homes.

Q. Will you clean up the leaf litter afterwards?

Debris is bagged and taken away — you won't be left with a pile of wet leaves under the downpipe.

Q. Can you install gutter guard as well?

Yes, mesh gutter guard can be supplied and fitted for homes that clog up every season.

Q. Do you service commercial properties?

Yes — shops, sheds and commercial roofs around Bunbury are covered too.
