How a job runs

Dirt out, base in, concrete down.

Because the same crew does the earthworks and the concrete, the job runs as one sequence instead of three separate bookings that never quite line up.

Stage 01

Strip & cut

Vegetation and topsoil off, levels cut to the set-out, spoil either spread on site or carted away.

Stage 02

Base & services

Trenches in for services, fill brought up in layers and compacted so the pad does not move later.

Stage 03

Form & pour

Formwork and mesh set, concrete placed and finished, then stripped and cleaned up behind us.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do the concrete as well, or just the dirt?

Both. That is the point of the business — the same crew cuts the site, preps the base and pours the slab or driveway, so you are not chasing two contractors.

Q. Do you go across the river into NSW?

Yes. Gol Gol, Dareton, Pomona, Balranald and Pooncarie are all regular work, along with the Victorian side around Mildura, Irymple, Red Cliffs and Merbein.

Q. Is the quote free?

Yes — quotes are free and in writing. You only pay once you have accepted a price and the job is booked in.

Q. Can you get into a tight backyard?

Usually. Send a photo of the access and the side gate and we will tell you honestly what will fit and what will not before anyone turns up.

Q. What happens to the dirt you dig out?

Depends on the job. Clean fill can often be spread or reused on site, which saves money. Anything that has to go gets carted away and that is priced up front.

Q. Do you take on rural and irrigation work?

Yes — track work, levelling, channel and pipeline trenching on blocks and farms out through the Sunraysia district.

Q. How small a job will you take?

Small jobs are fine — a shed pad, a path, a driveway, a few pier holes. It is worth asking rather than assuming it is too little to bother with.

Q. How far ahead do I need to book?

It moves with the weather and the run of work. Ring the mobile and you will get a straight answer on when we could start.
