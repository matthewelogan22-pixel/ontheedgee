Who's behind it

A CQ-based refrigeration & air con team that actually turns up.

Cool Climate Services is a HVAC and refrigeration contractor based in Gladstone Central, working across the Central Queensland region — the kind of outfit where the tech on your roof or in your cool room is the same person you spoke to on the phone.

The work spans breakdown repairs, new installations, and maintenance across air conditioning and refrigeration — covering residential, commercial, industrial and marine jobs, from a shop's display fridge to a vessel's cooling system.

Cool Climate Services deals directly with hospitality venues, retailers, tradies and homeowners across Gladstone, Tannum Sands, Boyne Island, Agnes Water, Calliope and Biloela — no call centre, no subcontracted crew you've never met.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you work on cool rooms and commercial fridges, or just air conditioning?

Both — Cool Climate Services covers air conditioning and refrigeration, from split systems and ducted AC through to cool rooms, freezer rooms and display fridges.

Q. Do you do emergency breakdown callouts?

Yes — if a cool room or AC system goes down, get in touch and Cool Climate Services will get back to you to arrange a callout.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Gladstone Central and the surrounding CQ region — including Tannum Sands, Boyne Island, Agnes Water, Calliope and Biloela. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. Do you handle industrial and marine refrigeration?

Yes — alongside residential and commercial work, Cool Climate Services services industrial and marine refrigeration and cooling systems.

Q. Do you give a price before starting work?

Yes — new split system and commercial AC installations, sized and fitted properly, alongside repairs and maintenance.

Q. How do I get a quote?
