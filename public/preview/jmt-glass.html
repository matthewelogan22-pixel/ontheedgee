Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do emergency glass repairs?

Yes — a broken or cracked window gets priority. Call the number above and describe what's happened; most jobs can be scheduled quickly.

Q. Can you replace glass for insurance claims?

Yes, JMT can quote and complete glass replacement work you can submit to your insurer.

Q. Do you do shower screens and mirrors as well as windows?

Yes — frameless and semi-frameless shower screens, custom mirrors, plus all standard window and door glazing.

Q. Do you supply and fit security doors and screens?

Yes, JMT is a local Amplimesh dealer and fits security doors and screens alongside general glazing work.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Mount Gambier and the surrounding Limestone Coast — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, call and ask anyway.

Q. Do you charge for quotes?

No. Measures and quotes are free, with no obligation to go ahead.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — all glazing work is completed to Australian glass standards and backed by workmanship warranty.

Q. Do you supply both aluminium and uPVC windows?

Yes — happy to talk through which suits your home and budget before you commit to either.
