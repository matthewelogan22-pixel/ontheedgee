Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you supply the tiles or do I choose my own?

Either — bring your own tiles from a local supplier, or Reece can point you toward suppliers and help you choose based on the space and budget.

Q. How far out of Launceston do you travel?

Remar Tiling services the North, North West and East of Tasmania — see the service area above. Call and ask if you're further out.

Q. Is the quote really free?

Yes — Reece measures the space and provides a written quote at no cost, with no obligation to book the job.

Q. How long does a typical bathroom take?

Most bathroom retiles take a few days depending on size and whether waterproofing and floor prep are needed — you'll get a realistic timeframe with the quote.

Q. Do you work with builders on new homes?

Yes — Remar Tiling regularly coordinates directly with builders on new-build tiling packages across the region.

Q. Can you fix a few cracked or lifted tiles without redoing the whole room?

Yes — small repair jobs are taken on, not just full retiles. Send a photo and Reece can usually give you a quick idea of cost.

Q. Do you handle waterproofing as part of the job?

Yes — wet areas are waterproofed to Australian Standard as a standard part of any bathroom or wet area retile.
