FAQ

Common questions

How often should windows be cleaned?

Most Bunbury homes look their best on a quarterly clean, while shopfronts and high-traffic commercial glass usually suit fortnightly or monthly visits.

Do you clean inside and out?

Yes — both are available, and most residential bookings include a full inside-and-out clean.

What's a pure water system?

Water is purified so it dries completely spot-free on the glass — no detergent residue, no streaks, and it's safe for use near gardens.
