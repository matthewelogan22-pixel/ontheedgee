Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you come to me, or do I bring the car in?

A.O.K comes to you — your home, workplace, station or worksite around Katherine. That's the whole point of a mobile mechanic.

Q. Can you issue a roadworthy certificate on the spot?

If the vehicle passes, yes. If something needs fixing first, you'll get a clear list and a price before any work goes ahead.

Q. Do you work on 4x4s and station vehicles, not just regular cars?

Yes — cars, 4x4s, bikes, trucks, trailers and plant equipment are all covered, which matters out here.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Katherine and the surrounding area — see the service area above. If you're on a property further out, call and ask.

Q. How quickly can you get to me?

Most jobs are booked within a few days; call and describe what's happening and A.O.K will give you a realistic time.

Q. Do you give a price before starting?

Always. Beyond the inspection or callout, nothing gets fixed without you knowing the price first.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — every job is backed by workmanship guarantee. If something's not right, A.O.K comes back and sorts it.

Q. Do you do auto electrical work too?

Yes — wiring faults, batteries and electrical diagnostics across cars, trucks and trailers.

Vehicle not going anywhere?

Call direct — no towing it into town, no waiting on a bay. Just A.O.K.
