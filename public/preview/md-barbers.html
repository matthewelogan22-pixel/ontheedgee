Who's behind it

A proper barbershop, not a salon dressed up as one.

MD Barbers has been cutting hair on Macarthur Street in Soldiers Hill for years — the kind of shop where regulars get remembered by name and walk-ins get looked after just the same.

Open through the week for the Soldiers Hill and wider Ballarat crowd — tradies before work, students after school, and blokes who've been coming in for years.

Before you come in

Common questions.

Q. Do I need to book ahead?

No — walk-ins are always welcome. If you'd rather lock in a time, just give the shop a call.

Q. How long's the wait usually?

Depends on the time of day — weekday mornings are quietest. Call ahead if you want a rough idea before heading in.

Q. Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes — patient, no-fuss cuts for younger clients, any age.

Q. Do you do hot towel shaves?

Yes, a proper traditional straight-razor hot towel shave — on its own or paired with a cut.

Q. What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm. Closed weekends.

Q. Where exactly are you located?

402 Macarthur Street, Soldiers Hill — a few minutes from Ballarat CBD.

Q. Can I request a specific barber?

Of course — let us know when you come in or call ahead and we'll do our best to sort it.

Q. Do you take cash and card?

Yes, both are fine.
