Standards

Scaffolding in NSW is licensed work.

What the rules actually require on a site — worth knowing before you book anyone's scaffold.

High Risk Work Licence

In NSW, any scaffold where a person or object could fall more than four metres must be erected, altered and dismantled by a scaffolder.

AS/NZS 4576

The Australian and New Zealand guidelines for scaffolding — how bays get tied, braced, boarded and handrailed.

Handover & scaftag

A scaffold should be inspected and tagged before anyone works off it, then re-inspected at least every 30 days while it stands.

Nobody alters it but the scaffolder

Planks moved or handrails pulled off by another trade voids the tag. If something needs changing, call — it gets changed properly and re-tagged.

Before you book

Questions builders actually ask.

Q. How much notice do you need?

The earlier the better, especially through the busy months. Ring with the slab or frame date and the erect gets pencilled in around your program instead of squeezed in later.

Q. How is scaffold priced?

Usually as one figure covering the erect, an initial hire period and the dismantle, then ongoing hire if the job runs longer. You get that number before anything is booked.

Q. Can the scaffold stay up longer than planned?

Yes — jobs run over, that's normal. Let us know and the hire simply continues. Nothing comes down while your trades still need it.

Q. What if we need an extra lift part way through?

Call and it gets altered. Nobody else should be adding lifts, moving planks or removing handrails — altering a scaffold is licensed work and it voids the handover tag.

Q. Do you handle tight or sloping blocks?

Plenty of Shoalhaven sites are on a slope or squeezed against a boundary. That's what the site measure is for — base plates, sole boards and bay layout get worked out before erect day, not on it.

Q. Do you go south of Nowra?

Yes — down through Sussex Inlet, Milton and Ulladulla, and north to Berry and Kiama. If you're outside that, call anyway and you'll get a straight answer on whether it's worth the run.

Q. Can homeowners hire scaffold directly?

Yes. Painting, rendering or gutter work on a two-storey home is a common job — you don't need to be a builder to book it in.

Q. What's the fastest way to get a price?

Call 0414 754 822, or email the address with a couple of photos of the house or site. Photos answer half the questions before anyone drives out.
