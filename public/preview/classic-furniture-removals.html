Before you book

Common questions.

Q. Do you do interstate moves?

Yes — Sydney, Canberra, Wagga and beyond, with backloading options that can help keep the cost down.

Q. What are your insurance details?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — send yours through and they'll appear here.

Q. Do you give a quote before the day?

Always. You'll have a firm price before the truck turns up — no surprise charges once you're loaded.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Young and the Hilltops region — Boorowa, Cowra, Harden, Grenfell and Cootamundra included. Just outside that? Call and ask anyway.

Q. How far ahead should I book?

Weekends and end-of-month fill fast for a small crew — a couple of weeks' notice is ideal, but call anyway if it's short notice.

Q. Do you supply packing materials?

Furniture blankets and wrap are included on every job, and boxes can be arranged if you need them.

Q. Can you move just a few items, not a whole house?

Of course — single pieces, a few boxes, a fridge on its own, Classic still turns up.

Q. Is there anything you can't move?

Most household goods are fine — just flag anything unusual (pianos, pool tables, safes) when booking so the right gear is on the truck.
