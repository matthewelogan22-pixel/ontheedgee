Who we are

A Dubbo cleaning crew, not a call centre.

Lords Cleaning Dubbo has been working in and around Dubbo for a decade. We're a local contract cleaning company — the same crew, the same standard, turning up on the same schedule so you stop having to think about it.

Most of what we do is commercial: offices, shopfronts, amenities, lunchrooms and common areas on a regular run, usually before or after hours so nobody's working around a mop. Being local matters in a town this size. If something's missed you call the person who runs the business, not a ticket queue in another state — and it gets sorted on the next run.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you clean outside business hours?

Yes — most commercial runs are done before opening or after close so your staff and customers aren't working around us. Timing is set when the contract is agreed.

Q. Is there a minimum contract?

No. Some clients are on a nightly run, others are fortnightly, and plenty of work is genuinely one-off — a builders clean or a vacate clean with no ongoing commitment.

Q. How does the quote work?

We walk the site, agree on exactly what's included, and give you a written price per run. Nothing is quoted sight-unseen, and there's no charge for the walk-through.

Q. Do you supply consumables?

That's a scope decision — some clients want toilet paper, hand towel and liners included in the run, others supply their own. Either works; it just gets written into the agreement.

Q. Can you handle government and council sites?

Yes — Lords Cleaning Dubbo is listed on the buy.nsw Supplier Hub, so public-sector work can be engaged through the standard NSW procurement channel.

Q. What if something gets missed?

Call the number on this page. It goes to the business, not a call centre, and it'll be corrected on the next run.

Q. Do you do bond cleans for tenants?

Yes — ovens, filters, cupboard tops, carpets, kitchens, bathrooms and floors, done against the list agents actually inspect against.

Q. How do we pay?

EFT, EFTPOS, Visa and Mastercard. Commercial clients are invoiced on an agreed cycle rather than paying per visit.

Want a price on your site?

Tell us the building, the rough size and how often you want it done. We'll walk through it and come back with a written scope and a fixed price per run.
