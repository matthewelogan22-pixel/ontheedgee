Before you book

Common questions.

Q. Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointment only — it's how every client gets proper, unrushed time in the chair. Call or message and a time will be found that suits.

Q. Will I see the same stylist every visit?

Yes, always Rachael. That's the whole point of a one-chair studio — no re-explaining what you want to someone new each time.

Q. Do you offer colour consultations?

Yes, free of charge, before any colour service — especially useful for a big change like going blonde or correcting previous colour.

Q. What products do you use?

Professional salon-grade colour and treatment ranges, chosen to keep colour looking good between visits, not just on the day.

Q. How far in advance should I book?

A week or two for a standard cut and colour, longer around Christmas and formal season when the books fill up fast.

Q. Do you do kids' cuts?

Yes — just mention it when booking so enough time is set aside.

Q. What are your hours?

By appointment, with some flexibility on days and evenings — get in touch and a time will be worked out.

Q. Can you fix colour done somewhere else?

Often, yes. Bring a photo of the current colour and what you're hoping for and it can be assessed before booking anything in.
