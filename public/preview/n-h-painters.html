Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do both interior and exterior painting?

Yes — full interiors, exteriors, or a mix of both. Multi-level and complex homes are no problem.

Q. Do you give a written quote before starting?

Always. Nev comes out, has a look, and gives you a firm price in writing before any paint is bought.

Q. Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured for residential painting work across Geelong and surrounds.

Q. Do you supply the paint, or do we?

N&H supplies trade-quality paint as standard, or works with a specific brand or colour you've already chosen.

Q. How long does a typical repaint take?

Depends on the size and prep needed — you'll get a realistic timeframe as part of the quote, not a vague estimate.

Q. Do you help with colour choices?

Yes — happy to talk through finishes and colours on-site, especially for tricky rooms or resale prep.

Q. Can you fit in end-of-lease repaints?

Yes, landlords and property managers can get fast turnarounds between tenants — just call ahead to lock in dates.

Q. What areas around Geelong do you cover?

Geelong and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. Just outside it? Call and ask anyway.
