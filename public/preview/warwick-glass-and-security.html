Common Questions

What people usually ask us.

Q. Do you make the security screens yourselves?

Yes — every door and window screen is measured on-site and made to fit your exact opening, not a standard off-the-shelf size.

Q. What's the difference between diamond mesh and stainless mesh screens?

Diamond mesh is a strong, cost-effective aluminium grille popular for everyday security, while stainless steel mesh gives a finer, harder-to-cut option for extra peace of mind — we'll talk you through what suits your home during the quote.

Q. Can you replace a single cracked or fogged-up window pane?

Yes, we cut and replace individual panes, including failed double-glazed units, without needing to replace the whole window frame.

Q. Do you travel outside Warwick itself?

We service the wider Southern Downs by appointment, including Allora, Killarney, Stanthorpe, Clifton and surrounding towns — get in touch and we'll confirm your area.

Q. Is the quote really free?

Yes — we measure and quote on-site with no obligation to go ahead. You only pay once you've decided to book the job in.

Q. Do you do shower screens and mirrors too, or just security and glazing?

Both — framed and frameless shower screens, splashback mirrors and robe doors are all part of what we cut and fit.
