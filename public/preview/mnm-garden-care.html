Through the year

What needs doing, and when.

Wagga grows hard in spring then bakes. What a yard needs in October is not what it needs in January.

Sep – Nov

Growth

Grass takes off once the frosts finish. Weekly or fortnightly cuts right through spring.

Dec – Feb

Heat

Forty-degree stretches. Cut higher so the roots stay shaded, and water deep rather than often.

Mar – May

Recovery

Growth eases off. Best window of the year for hedging, pruning and a proper clean-out of the beds.

Jun – Aug

Slow

Frost most mornings. Barely any mowing — good time for structural pruning and getting on top of weeds.
