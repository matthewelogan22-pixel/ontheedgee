Who's behind it

Cairns North's early-start local barbershop.

North Cairns Barber Shop opens its doors on Sheridan Street before most of Cairns has had its first coffee — 7am weekdays, 8am on Sundays, so tradies, shift workers and school kids can get sorted before the day starts.

Skin fades, tapers, scissor cuts, beard trims and hot towel shaves — quick enough for a lunch break, thorough enough that you'll come back for the next one.

No booking system, no appointment app — just a chair, a good barber, and a straightforward cut done properly in the Far North Queensland heat.

Before you come in

Common questions.

Q. What time do you open?

7am on weekdays, 8am on Sundays — early enough to fit around work or school, best to call ahead over public holidays.

Q. Do I need to book?

No — walk-ins are always welcome. Early mornings tend to be quietest.

Q. Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes — easy, patient cuts for younger clients of any age.

Q. Do you do hot towel shaves?

Yes — a traditional hot towel shave, on its own or paired with a cut.

Q. Where exactly are you?

321 Sheridan Street, Cairns North — easy to spot, easy to park nearby.

Q. Are you open on weekends?

Yes, 7 days a week — Sundays from 8am.

Q. Can I request a specific barber?

Sure — just ask when you come in and we'll do our best to sort it.

Q. Do you take cash and card?

Yes, both are fine.
