Before you message

Common questions.

Q. Do you do both heating and cooling?

Yes — split systems, ducted heating and cooling, gas heaters, and evaporative cooling. Most Bendigo homes need both, and it's quoted as one job where it makes sense.

Q. Why don't you have a website?

Word of mouth and Facebook have carried the business for years — but everything you'd want to know (services, area, contact) is right here.

Q. Is the quote really free?

Yes, no obligation. Send a message with the job details or a photo and you'll get a straight answer, not a sales pitch.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Bendigo and Central Victoria — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, send a message and ask anyway.

Q. How quickly can you get to a job?

Depends on the season — heating call-outs get busy in June/July and cooling in December/January. Message early and you'll get an honest timeframe, not a vague one.

Q. Do you charge just to come and quote?

No. Quotes are free and obligation-free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and booked the work in.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — every install and repair is backed by a workmanship warranty.

Q. How often should my system be serviced?

Once a year is the general rule — ideally heating before winter and cooling before summer — to catch small issues before they become a breakdown.
