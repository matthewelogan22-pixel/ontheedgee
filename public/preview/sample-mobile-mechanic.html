Before you book.

Can you really do a service in my driveway?

Yes. Services, brakes, batteries and most diagnostics only need a flat, safe spot to park. Jobs that need a hoist, like a clutch or a gearbox, go to a workshop, and we'll tell you that before you book.

Will a mobile service void my new-car warranty?

Generally, no. The ACCC says manufacturer warranties typically let you choose your own repairer, as long as the servicing meets the manufacturer's standards. We follow the logbook schedule and stamp the book. Some warranties do send warranty repairs back to the dealer, so it's worth checking yours.

How do I get a price?

Text 0491 570 156 with the make, model and year, plus what it's doing, and we'll text back a price. If something else turns up once we're working on it, you get a photo and a price, and we wait for your yes.

Do I need to be there?

Not for most jobs. Plenty of people leave the car at work and the keys at reception. We text you when it's done.
