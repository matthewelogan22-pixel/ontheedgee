What I do

The work, plainly.

No packages, no upsells. Tell me what's happening and I'll tell you what it takes to fix it.

01

Switchboards

Old fuse boards swapped for modern boards with breakers and RCDs. Tested on completion, and you get the paperwork rather than a verbal "she's right".

02

Rewiring

Full or partial rewires. Can be staged room by room if you'd rather not have the whole house opened up at once.

03

Safety switches

RCDs fitted and tested. If your board still runs on fuses, there's nothing standing between a fault and whoever's holding the appliance.

04

Lighting & power

LED conversions, downlights, outdoor and security lighting, extra outlets, ceiling and exhaust fans.

05

Fault finding

Breaker tripping for no obvious reason, or half the house dead. The job that takes patience rather than parts.
