Who's behind it

A Bathurst stone business, not a call centre.

Naked Stone And Co. is a stone benchtop business based at Kelso, just east of Bathurst, run by Sam Gibb with more than ten years in the trade. The person who quotes your job is the person who templates it and the person who carries it through your door.

Most of the work is kitchens — new builds alongside local cabinetmakers and builders, and renovations where a tired laminate top finally gets swapped for stone. Vanities, laundry tops, islands, splashbacks and bar tops all go through the same workshop.

If you've already bought your slab somewhere else, that's fine too — install-only is a service here, not a favour. And if an existing top has a chip or a crack in it, it gets repaired rather than replaced wherever the stone allows.

Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Can you install stone I've bought somewhere else?

Yes — install-only is one of the main services. Whether the slab came from a supplier, a kitchen company or a previous job, it gets templated, cut and fitted the same way.

Q. My benchtop is chipped. Does the whole top have to come out?

Usually not. Chips, cracked cut-outs around sinks and cooktops, and failed joins can often be repaired in place. Send a photo through and you'll get an honest answer on whether it's worth repairing or replacing.

Q. How far do you travel?

Bathurst, Kelso, Orange, Mudgee, Lithgow, Cowra, Oberon, Parkes, Forbes, Canowindra and Carcoar are all regular runs. Anywhere else in the Central West, call and ask.

Q. Do you work with cabinetmakers and builders?

Yes — a good share of the work is trade. Measure, fabricate and fit to your program so the kitchen install isn't held up waiting on a top trucked in from the city.

Q. When does the benchtop get measured?

After the cabinetry is installed and level. That's what the stone gets cut to, so templating before the benches are in is exactly how gaps happen.

Q. Kitchens only, or other rooms too?

Kitchens, bathroom vanities, laundry tops, islands, bar tops, splashbacks, shelves and window sills — anywhere a stone surface belongs.

Q. How do I get a price?

Call 0412 973 093, or email through a cabinet plan, a sketch or a photo with rough measurements. That's enough for a ballpark before anyone drives out.
