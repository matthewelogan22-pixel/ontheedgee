Before you call

Common questions.

Do you handle everything, or just laying pavers?

Everything from excavation and base compaction through to the final paving — the ground gets prepped properly first.

How much does a driveway cost?

It depends on size, ground condition and paver choice — Blue Valley measures on-site and gives a firm written price, free of charge.

Can you fix pavers that have sunk or shifted?

Yes — sunken or uneven areas can usually be lifted, re-based and relaid rather than replaced entirely.

What areas do you cover?

Mount Gambier and the surrounding South East. If you're just outside it, get in touch anyway.

How long does a driveway take?

Most residential driveways take a few days depending on size and how much excavation is needed — you'll get a realistic timeframe with the quote.

Do you charge for the initial quote?

No — the on-site measure and quote are free with no obligation to go ahead.

What paver types do you work with?

Brick, concrete and natural stone pavers, matched to what suits the property and budget.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — work is backed by a workmanship guarantee. If a paver moves that shouldn't have, Blue Valley comes back and fixes it.
