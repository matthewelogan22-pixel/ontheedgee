Before you book

Common questions.

Q. Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from small breeds needing a light trim through to large double-coated dogs needing a full de-shed. Let Darlene know the breed when booking so enough time is set aside.

Q. How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well on a 6-8 week cycle, though heavy-shedding or fast-growing coats can need it more often. Darlene will suggest a schedule after the first visit.

Q. Is Larbellah a shop or a private studio?

It's a small, private appointment-only space in Riverside — not a busy retail-shop groomer, so there's no other dogs milling about while yours is being worked on.

Q. My dog gets anxious at the groomer — can you still help?

That's a big part of why owners choose Larbellah. Sessions run at a calmer pace for nervous or first-time dogs, with breaks if needed.

Q. How far ahead do I need to book?

Usually within the same week, sometimes sooner if there's a cancellation. Popular time slots book out faster around holidays.

Q. What areas do you service?

Riverside, Legana, Trevallyn, Newnham, Mowbray, Kings Meadows and the wider Launceston and West Tamar area.

Ready to book your dog in?

Call, text or email Darlene directly — no booking system to fight with, just a real reply from the person doing the grooming.
