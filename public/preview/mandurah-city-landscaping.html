Coastal conditions

Why Peel yards behave differently.

Three things make a Mandurah block harder than an inland one, and all three are worth designing around rather than fighting.

Soil

Sand that won't hold water

Coastal sand drains almost immediately and turns hydrophobic in summer. Without soil improvement and wetting agent, you're watering straight past the roots.

Wind

Salt on the sea breeze

The afternoon breeze carries salt well inland. Plants that shrug it off stay; the ones that don't get burnt edges and get replaced every couple of seasons.

Summer

Long, dry and bright

Months of heat with little rain. Turf variety, mulch depth and bed placement matter far more here than they do an hour inland.

Questions

Common questions.

What turf works best around Mandurah?

It depends on how much sun and traffic the lawn gets. The bigger factor is soil preparation — good turf on unprepared coastal sand still struggles.

Can you work with a sloped block?

Yes. Retaining and terracing is a large part of the work around here, and it's usually what makes a difficult block usable.
