Before you call

Common questions.

Q. How much does a quote cost?

Nothing. We come out, look at the yard, and give you a written quote before anything is booked in.

Q. How long does a typical job take?

Depends on the scope — a turf refresh might be a day, a full yard renovation can run one to two weeks. You'll get a timeframe with your quote.

Q. Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes — one team from planning through to planting, paving, or laying turf. No handing off to a separate contractor.

Q. What areas do you service?

Mandurah and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, get in touch anyway.
