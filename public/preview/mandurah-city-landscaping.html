Before you call

Common questions.

Q. How much does a quote cost?

Nothing. We come out, look at the yard, and give you a written quote before anything is booked in.

Q. How long does a typical job take?

Depends on the scope — a turf refresh might be a day, a full yard renovation can run one to two weeks. You'll get a timeframe with your quote.

Q. Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes — one team from planning through to planting, paving, or laying turf. No handing off to a separate contractor.

Q. What areas do you service?

Mandurah and the surrounding suburbs — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, get in touch anyway.

Q. Do you supply the turf and plants, or do I need to organise that?

We handle supply as part of the job — turf, soil, plants, pavers, all sourced and included in the quote.

Q. Can you work with an existing garden, or only full rebuilds?

Both. Plenty of jobs are a refresh or partial redesign around what's already there, not a full teardown.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — turf and planting are guaranteed to establish properly. If something doesn't take, we come back and sort it.

Q. Do I need to be home for the quote?

It helps, but not always — if you can give access to the yard, a walk-through works fine.
