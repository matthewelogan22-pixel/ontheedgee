Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do emergency callouts?

Yes — most jobs are same-day. Send a message and describe what's happening; genuine emergencies (burst pipes, no hot water, major leaks) get priority.

Q. What are your licence and insurance details?

Licence and insurance details aren't listed yet — send yours through and they'll appear here.

Q. Do you give quotes before starting?

Always. You'll know the price before any work begins — no surprise call-out fees or after-the-fact charges.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Rockhampton and Central Queensland — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, message and ask anyway.

Q. How quickly can you get here?

Most callouts are same-day. For non-urgent work, Weston will give you a realistic window when you book.

Q. Do you charge a call-out fee just to quote?

No. Quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the work is booked in.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — every job is finished to AS/NZS 3500 and backed by workmanship warranty. If something's not right, Weston comes back and fixes it.

Q. Do you handle gas fitting as well as water plumbing?

Yes — gas connections and compliance work alongside general plumbing and drainage.
