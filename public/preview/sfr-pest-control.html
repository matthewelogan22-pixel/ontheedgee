Before you call

Common questions.

Q. How fast can you get out for a spider or rodent problem?

Turnaround is usually quick for local Wagga jobs — call or message with what you're seeing and SFR will give you a realistic timeframe.

Q. Are the treatments safe for kids and pets?

Products used are APVMA-registered and applied according to label directions — SFR will talk you through any precautions for your specific property before treating.

Q. Do you do one-off treatments or ongoing pest control?

Both — a one-off treatment for a specific problem, or a regular service to keep spiders, flies and rodents from coming back season after season.

Q. What areas do you cover?

Wagga Wagga and the surrounding Riverina — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, message and ask anyway.

Q. Do you quote before starting work?

Always. You'll get a firm price before any treatment begins — no surprise charges after the fact.

Q. Do you handle commercial properties?

Yes — shops, offices and sheds across the Riverina get the same targeted treatment approach as residential jobs.

Q. How long does a treatment take?

Most residential treatments are wrapped up within an hour or two, depending on the size of the property and what's being treated.

Q. How do I book?

Call 0482 061 267 or send a message through the SFR Pest Control Facebook page — whichever's easier.
