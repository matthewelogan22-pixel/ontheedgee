Questions

Before you message us.

Do you hire out skip bins, or do you do the removal?

We're a full-service junk removal outfit — no bin dropped off for you to fill yourself. We turn up, load everything, and take it away in the one visit.

How is a quote worked out?

Mostly on how much you need cleared. Send a photo or two through Facebook Messenger and we'll give you a straight price before we book anything in.

Can you do same-day pickups?

Often, yes — depends on what's already booked in that day. Message us and we'll tell you the soonest we can get there.

What can't you take?

Asbestos and hazardous chemicals need a specialist. Everything else — general household, garden, and reno waste — we're set up to handle.

Do you cover areas outside Mount Gambier?

Yes — we regularly get out to Compton, Moorak, Yahl, Millicent and the wider Limestone Coast. Just ask when you message us.

Do I need to be home for the job?

Not always — if it's a straightforward pile or agreed items, we can often arrange access without you needing to be on site.
