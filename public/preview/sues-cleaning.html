Before you book

Common questions.

Q. How much does a quote cost?

Nothing. A quick message with a few details on the space gets you a fixed price before anything is booked in.

Q. Do I need to supply cleaning products?

No — products and equipment are brought along. If you prefer specific products used in your home, just mention it.

Q. Can you do a one-off clean, or only ongoing?

Both. One-off deep cleans, end of lease, and move in/out cleans are just as welcome as ongoing weekly or fortnightly visits.

Q. Do you clean offices as well as homes?

Yes — office cleaning is scheduled before or after hours so it doesn't interrupt the workday.

Q. Will the same person clean each time?

Yes — consistency matters, so it's the same cleaner on your property visit to visit, not a rotating roster.

Q. What areas do you service?

Shepparton and the surrounding area — see the service area above. If you're just outside it, message and ask anyway.

Q. Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not necessarily — plenty of clients provide access and go about their day. Whatever you're comfortable with works.

Q. Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — if something's missed, let Sue know and it gets sorted, no argument.
