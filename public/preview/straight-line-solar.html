Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you only work in Deniliquin?

Deniliquin's home base, but the travelling team covers the wider Riverina and crosses over into VIC for the right job — call and ask if you're not sure you're in range.

Q. Do you do battery storage as well as panels?

Yes — battery add-ons for existing systems, or batteries bundled into a new install from the start.

Q. How long does an install take?

Most residential installs are done in a day, depending on roof size and system complexity. You'll get a realistic timeframe with your quote.

Q. Do you give quotes before starting?

Always. You'll know the price before any work begins — no surprise call-out fees or after-the-fact charges.

Q. Can you fix an existing system that isn't performing?

Yes — fault finding and repairs on systems that have dropped offline or aren't producing what they should.

Q. Do you handle rural and farm properties?

Yes — larger arrays for sheds, pumps and rural power needs are part of the regular workload across the Riverina.

Q. Is there a call-out fee just to quote?

No. Quotes are free — you only pay once you've accepted a price and the work is booked in.

Q. How do I get in touch?

Call or email direct — see the contact details below. It goes straight to Josh, not a booking queue.
