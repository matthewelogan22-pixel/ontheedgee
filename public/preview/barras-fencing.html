Before you call

Common questions.

Q. Do you do Colorbond and timber fencing?

Yes — Colorbond panel fencing and gates, plus timber paling and slat-style screens. Barra will talk you through which suits your block and budget.

Q. Can you fence a sloped block or one with a retaining wall?

Yes — stepped or racked fencing on sloped ground, and fence-lines built on top of retaining walls, are regular jobs.

Q. Do you do pool fencing?

Yes, Colorbond and framed pool fencing. Let Barra know it's for a pool when you call so the job's measured with that in mind.

Q. Is the quote free?

Always. Barra comes out, measures the fence-line, and gives you a firm price before anything's booked in — no cost, no obligation.

Q. What areas do you cover?

North Albury and the surrounding Albury-Wodonga area — see the service area above. Just outside it? Call and ask anyway.

Q. Can you replace a fence that's already up?

Yes — old fencing is removed and cleared away as part of the job, and the new fence-line is set out fresh.

Q. How soon can you start?

Depends on the current job list — call for a realistic timeframe rather than a guess.

Q. Do you install gates on their own, without a full fence job?

Yes — new gates or repairs to an existing sagging or sticking gate can be booked on their own.
