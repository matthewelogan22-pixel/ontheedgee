About Cohoes

A Toowoomba institution, still run the same way.

Cohoes has been cutting keys and fixing locks on Prescott Street since 1954 — long enough to have re-keyed half of Toowoomba City at least once. It's the kind of local trade where regulars just walk in, and new customers get treated the same way by the time they leave.

No call centre, no franchise scripts — just a locksmith's counter, a key-cutting bench, and someone who can usually sort a lockout, a lost car key or a tricky old mortice lock without you needing to explain it twice.

Common questions

What people ask before they call.

Q. Can you get me back in if I'm locked out?

In most cases, yes — non-destructive entry is tried first for both homes and businesses around Toowoomba City.

Q. Do you cut keys while I wait?

Standard and most security keys are cut on the spot at the Prescott St counter.

Q. Can you re-key my locks instead of replacing them?

Yes — re-keying is usually cheaper than new hardware and works well after a move-in or a lost key.

Q. Do you do car keys?

Cohoes cuts and supplies keys for a wide range of vehicles, including older models.

Q. Do you handle commercial master key systems?

Yes — from a small office to a multi-tenant building with tiered access.

Q. What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:10pm. Closed weekends.
